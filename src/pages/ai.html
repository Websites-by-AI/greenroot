Meet Raha

Raha (رهاء — "freedom") is GreenRoot's AI recovery companion: a craving coach in the hard moments, a mood tracker between sessions, and a career guide that leads you back to the job you had before addiction.

"Cravings last about 20 minutes. You never have to face one alone."

What Raha does

Six jobs, one companion

🆘

Craving SOS

Urge-surfing coach, breathing exercises and a delay timer — plus one-tap call to your buddy or counselor when it's too much.

📊

Mood & relapse watch

Daily 30-second check-ins. Raha spots risky patterns — bad sleep, missed shifts, isolation — and alerts your counselor (only if you opt in).

🪙

Token wallet & goals

See tokens earned per shift, set goals (a course, a bicycle, family gifts) and celebrate milestones — small wins, wired into the habit loop.

💼

Career rewind

The signature feature: Raha maps the job you had before addiction, finds your skill gaps, builds a refresh plan and practises interviews with you.

👨‍👩‍👧

Family bridge

Opt-in updates for family: milestones reached, shifts worked, mood trends — rebuilding trust with facts, not promises.

🔐

Private by design

Your data belongs to you. Encrypted, opt-in for every share, never shared with police or employers. Delete everything with one tap.

Safety & honesty

  • Raha is an assistant, not a therapist — licensed humans make clinical decisions.

  • This demo is scripted and stores nothing.
  • The production version would require clinical validation and strict data-protection review (GDPR and local law).
