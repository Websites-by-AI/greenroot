Sources, limits & a safe first approach

Names and contact routes come from official websites and public institutional directories. Original Turkish and Persian names are preserved. The check date records our review, not the source’s last update.

A public line can be a switchboard, a branch line or a domestic call centre. Ask for the relevant team. Do not use emergency or counselling hotlines for sales.

Researched leads, not endorsements, agreements or an exhaustive census. Clinical licensing, foreign-founder eligibility, work permits and funding eligibility need local professional checks. GreenRoot is a concept prototype; other site metrics are demo data.

Keep outreach individual and respectful. Do not send patient names or records. Clinical care must be independent of work output; pilots need lawful contracts, real wages and professional safety oversight.
