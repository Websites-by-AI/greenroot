FAQ

Honest questions, honest answers

Is this real employment or just "therapy theater"?

Real employment. Participants sign a contract, earn at or above local minimum wage, and tokens are additional rewards on top — never a replacement for pay.

What if I relapse during the program?

You keep your place. Relapse is part of how addiction works medically. We pause the work, restart clinical support, and return when your health team agrees. You are not fired for being sick.

Do I have to quit my medication to join?

No — the opposite. Medication-assisted treatment is funded and encouraged where medically indicated. Our program coordinates with your doctor.

Will the AI report me to anyone?

Never without your consent. Raha's data belongs to you. Only opt-in alerts to your counselor exist, and crisis escalations go to humans who care for you — not to authorities.

Can I really go back to my old career?

In many cases, yes. Raha's career-rewind plan rebuilds skills honestly, frames the gap truthfully, and arranges supported internships with partner employers who hire for ability, not history.

Which cities does the pilot run in?

We are scoping pilot cohorts in Frankfurt and Tehran. Sign up and we'll tell you what's active near you.

🆘 In crisis right now?

If you or someone near you is in immediate danger (overdose, suicidal thoughts), contact your local emergency number now. In Iran, the state social-emergency addiction hotline is 09628. In Germany, you can call 112 for emergencies. You matter. One call can start everything.
