Why GreenRoot exists

Three crises, one root

Addiction, unemployment and waste feed each other. GreenRoot attacks all three at once.

🌀

Addiction thrives in emptiness

Recovery fails when days are empty — no structure, no purpose, no people. Most relapses happen in long, unstructured hours alone.

🔒

The job trap

Employers reject gaps in a CV, so people in recovery stay jobless — and joblessness pushes them back to use. The door stays closed from both sides.

🗑️

Cities are drowning in waste

Parks, riverbanks and streets collect litter faster than cities can clean it. Meanwhile recyclable material worth millions is lost every year.

Our answer

One program, three engines

Each engine makes the other two stronger. That is the whole idea.

🧠

From street to ward

Waste in, medicine out

The trash participants collect literally becomes part of their cure — twice.

💰

1. Recycling income funds treatment

Sold recyclables — PET, metal, paper, glass — generate revenue. That revenue pays for therapy sessions, psychiatric care and essential medicines for the crew. Every kilo collected is a kilo of treatment funded.

🩺

2. Materials become medical supplies

In our upcycling line, collected plastics are cleaned, shredded and re-manufactured — with certified partners — into medical products: face shields, splint components and sterile-grade packaging for clinics.
